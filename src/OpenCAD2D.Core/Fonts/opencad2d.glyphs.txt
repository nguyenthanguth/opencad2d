# opencad2d.glyphs.txt: the skeleton of OpenCAD2D ISO, the built-in single-stroke font (OpenCad2dIso.cs reads it).
#
# One glyph or mark a line, UTF-8 without a BOM, LF line ends. A line starting with # is a comment.
#
#   glyph <hex> <char> w=<ink width> [adv=<advance>] [top=<x>] [bot=<x>] : <path>
#   mark  <name> : <path>
#
# Units are d, the line width of ISO 3098 type B: the capitals' height h is 10 d, so one decimal is one font unit (a
# unit is d / 10, the capitals reach 100 of them). x runs right and y up; a glyph's ink starts at x 0 (the generator
# adds the left bearing of 1 d) and a mark is drawn about its anchor, x 0, from the bottom of its zone, y 0.
#
# <hex> is the code point, <char> only a label. w is the ink width; the generator fails where the ink does not run from
# 0 to w (a glyph with adv, the digits, only has to lie inside 0 to w, w being their cell). adv is the advance where it
# is not w + 2 d. top and bot are the x of the anchor of marks above and below (w / 2 and top where not given).
#
# A path is ops separated by spaces:
#   M x,y        lift the pen and move there, a stroke begins
#   L x,y        a line to there
#   A+ cx,cy x,y an arc counter-clockwise about (cx, cy) from the pen to (x, y); A- clockwise
#   O cx,cy,r    a whole circle, a stroke of its own
#   D x,y        a dot: a circle of radius 0.3 at x, y (left out of the ink box)
# A middle dot or a bar between strokes is only for reading. Every point and centre lies on the 0.1 grid.
